**2. Purpose and audience**  
Tilely is a free Turkish pattern-coloring game for preschool children and students in grades 1 and 2. It gives early learners a place to practice visual matching, colors and mirror symmetry. Players reproduce a sample by painting a grid. In symmetry levels, they complete the missing half. Each of the three stages has 300 levels.

**3. Setup and access**  
No account, credentials, sample files, payment or internet connection are required. Launch Tilely and tap **Okul öncesi** (Preschool), **1. sınıf** (Grade 1), or **2. sınıf** (Grade 2). Open the first level from the level list, select a palette color and tap or drag to paint cells. In the first Preschool level, **Artı** (Plus), color the five squares of the red plus. The game recognizes completion automatically in this level. In levels with manual checking, tap **Kontrol et** (Check). The grid icon returns to the level list, and **Kademeler** returns to stage selection. Hints, undo and clear are available within a level. Progress stays on the device. There are no account flows, user content uploads, advertising, or paid features.

**4. External services and tools**  
Tilely uses Capacitor and Apple's WKWebView to run its bundled HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Levels and artwork ship inside the app. There is no backend, analytics, external data provider, authentication service, payment processor or AI service involved in gameplay.

**5. Regional differences**  
The same Turkish content and features are available in every region where Tilely is distributed. Gameplay does not depend on location.

**6. Regulated services and protected material**  
Tilely is an educational puzzle game and does not provide regulated services. The game uses its own level and pattern artwork. Capacitor is an open source dependency.
